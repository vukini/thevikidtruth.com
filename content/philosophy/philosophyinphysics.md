# Philosophy in Physics

**The Philosophy of Physics is a very challenging field because not only do you have to be good at Philosophy,
you have to be good at Physics and Mathematics too!**

To tell you the truth, I try hard, but even this superhero has trouble here. **I feel this is a subject for
the gods**. Us mere mortals will just have to struggle - but there is joy in the struggle too. I hope that
my pages here will help shed some light on the subjects at hand.

I have tried to do two main things here:

1. I have tried to address subjects as clearly as I can with the least possible verbiage. I wan to keep things succinct.
    There are plenty of texts to get your feet wet in. I don't need to replicate these efforts.
1. I will add meaning from the perspective of this **Superhero**. In many ways, what is written here is how this philosophy
    is meaningful to me.

Once again, I don't want to repeat myself but I must reiterate:

- **I am no Academic**
- **The texts are not comprehensive you will be required to read around and outside the text to make sense of it**
- **I will not try and put caveats between each line and hedge each word. You will have to interpret these texts
    charitably**.
