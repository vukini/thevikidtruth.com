# VikiD Philosophy

![Mind-2](/assets/mind-2.jpg)
{.center}

To me, **Philosophy** is the a body of work that encompasses the highest values of
all human thought.

It is the [rubric](rubric.md) that envelopes everything from **Art, Religion,
Science, Ehtics, Politics and beyond.** What exactly philosophy is not a trivial question to answer
but it needs answering. It is not something I am looking forward to doing. It is a
question that I have been meaning to write about for many years and with every
attempt, I have failed.

**So, please forgive me for I know not what I do.**

My latest
attempt is given below. Here is the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy
Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy
Wikipedia) article on the subject.

## What is Philosophy?

We must start in the **middle** and this is a problem. We have to start with what
we know, or think we know, for it may be wrong and subject to revision.

We must understand that we are **human beings** with a limited outlook. How we go
from here is the question that will ultimately give us a workable answer.

To answer this question I think it is better to think about what came before it.

We must cast our minds back evolutionarily to the beginnings of thought itself.
