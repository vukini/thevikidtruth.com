## How this board works

I'm VikiD, and this is the short daily look at the numbers that sit underneath a cable drum, a coil of galvanised wire or a tonne of rebar on a Gulf site. Metals set the cost of the material. Oil and gas set the region's budgets and its project pipeline. Rates and the dollar set the cost of money, and the dirham is pegged to the dollar.

Every number comes from a free public source and links to it, with the date it is as of. Futures prices are the latest quote when the board was refreshed, not an official settlement. Some things have no free daily feed: copper and steel wire rod, Gulf cement and Gulf rebar. For those I show the best public reference I can find, such as a monthly US producer price index or the Shanghai rebar contract, and I say so. If a number can't be fetched, it says "unavailable". I never fill the gap with a guess.

Each daily note covers what moved, then **The truth:** what it means for UAE cable, electrical and construction suppliers. It's not investment advice.
