**The truth:** Oil down about 12% in a week is the headline, and I wouldn't read too much into one week. If it holds, it eases freight and energy costs for suppliers, but it also trims Gulf budget comfort over time. Copper is softer on the month, which is some relief on conductor costs, but a falling price makes buyers wait, so quotes may stay cautious. HRC up about 8% on the month means steel-based lines like wire, mesh, fencing and rebar still need short quote validity. Yields are well down on the month and the dirham is pegged, so financing isn't getting harder. Not investment advice.
