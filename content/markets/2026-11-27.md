**The truth:** Today most things bounced. Copper, aluminium, oil and gold are all up, but copper is still down about 16% in a month. One good day doesn't change that. Quotes for cable, wire and conductor-heavy electrical lines still need short validity both ways. Brent near $92 is well below a month ago, so Gulf budgets have a bit less cushion, though it's no crisis.
