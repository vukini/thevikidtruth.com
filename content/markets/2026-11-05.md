**The truth:** Oil is the story today. Brent losing almost a tenth of its price in one session, and about 16% in a week, is a big move, and I don't know yet if it sticks. In the short run it means cheaper freight, fuel and energy, which helps anyone moving drums of cable or steel. But at the Gulf level, oil under $95 is a lot less comfortable than $113 was a week ago, and if it stays there, that's the number to watch for government and project budgets next year. Dubai's market jumping 4% on the same day says local investors aren't worried yet, and I'd take that as a decent sign for the construction pipeline, not a promise. Copper has stopped falling for now, so the cushion on conductor costs is still there but not growing. Steel eased a touch but is still up about 7% on the month, so keep steel-heavy quotes short and keep escalation clauses in. Gold's jump tells you someone's nervous. Not investment advice.
