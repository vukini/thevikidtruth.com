Cheaper conductor metal helps buyers, but a fall this fast means quotes need short validity in both directions. I wouldn't call a bottom. Gold and global stocks fell hard too, so this looks like a wider risk-off move, not a metals story alone. Oil near $90 is well below a month ago, so Gulf budgets have less cushion, though it's not a crisis.
