**The truth:** This was a rough week everywhere except the Gulf. Copper is down about 17% in a month, aluminium about 10% in a week, and Friday's US stock drop was the sharpest of the run. Gold fell too, so this looks like a broad risk-off move, not a metals story alone. Cheaper conductor metal helps buyers, but a fall this fast means quotes need short validity in both directions. I wouldn't call a bottom. Oil near $90 is well below a month ago, so Gulf budgets have less cushion, though it's not a crisis. HRC is steady while rebar softens, so steel-based lines like wire, mesh, fencing and rebar are mixed. Ten-year yields around 5% and a firm dollar keep project financing dear. The dirham is pegged, so local rates follow. Dubai and Abu Dhabi are holding up much better than global stocks. I'd watch whether that lasts. Not investment advice.
