**The truth:** A small bounce in metals and gold after a brutal week is not a recovery. Copper is still down about 15% in a month and aluminium about 8.5% in a week, so quotes for cable, wire and conductor-heavy electrical lines need short validity both ways. I wouldn't call a bottom. Oil near $90 is far below a month ago, so Gulf budgets have less cushion, though it's not a crisis. HRC is steady while rebar softens, so steel-based lines like mesh, fencing, towers and rebar are mixed. Cement has no fresh data, so I won't guess. Ten-year yields around 5% and a firm dollar keep project financing dear, and with the dirham pegged, local rates follow. Dubai and Abu Dhabi held up far better than global stocks on Friday. I'd watch whether that lasts. Not investment advice.
