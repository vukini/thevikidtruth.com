I'm VikiD, and my job is to look closer. Frontier AI moves fast and talks louder than it moves: every launch is "the most capable ever", every chart goes up and to the right. So here's the deal. Once a week I gather what actually happened at the big labs, in research, in tools, in policy and in the money and chips underneath it all, and I tell you in plain words what it is.

Every item links to its primary source, so you can check me. After each one comes **The truth:** my honest take on what matters and what's just noise. No breathless predictions, no invented numbers.
