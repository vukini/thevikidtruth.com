# [The Laws of Physics](thelawsofphysics.md)

**There are no laws of Physics** - We stipulate that something must not change. Otherwise all *measurement is impossible*.
The Measuring Stick, the background against what we measure, must be 'fixed' in some way. We call this 'fixed' thing
**reality** and the **rules of interaction**, **laws**.
