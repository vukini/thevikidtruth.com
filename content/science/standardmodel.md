# The Standard Model

It is a **simple and comprehensive** theory that attempts to explain all
the hundreds of particles and complex interactions with only:

- 6 quarks
- 6 leptons
- Force carrier particles

All matter particles are composites of quarks and leptons, and they
interact by exchanging force carrier particles only. The theory has many detractors. It doesn't and cant explain
gravity for example.

I use the words **simple** in the sense that **other theories are either more complex or not as
comprehensive**.

[Simple is not easy](simpleisnoteasy.md "MISSING PAGE") however. The mathematics involved here are highly complex.
