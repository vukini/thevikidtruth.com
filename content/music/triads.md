# Triads

```
   Inversion 0 |  Inversion 1 |  Inversion 2

   A   C#  E   |  C#  E   A   |  E   A   C#

   B   D#  F#  |  D#  F#  D   |  F#  B   D#

   C   E   G   |  E   G   C   |  G   C   E

   D   F#  A   |  F#  A   D   |  A   D   F#

   E   G#  B   |  G#  B   E   |  B   E   G#

   F   A   C   |  A   C   F   |  C   F    A

   G   B   D   |  B   D   G   |  D   G    B
```

Learn the 7th Chords in Solfege. Chords are just stacked 3rds.

   Mi    So    Ti    Re

   Fa    La    Do    Mi

   So    Ti    Re    Fa
