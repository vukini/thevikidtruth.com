# Elenor Rigby

This is a truly beautiful song exhibiting both simplicity & complexity in its final form. See the links at
the bottom of the page to see how complex it can get.
I am now trying to play this song on the guitar, and possibly on the harmonica as well.
I will follow this process:

1. Learn the Lyrics of the Song First
1. Learn to Sing the Song with the correct intonation & rhythm
1. Learn the Chords required, practicing any complex changes
1. Play the whole song on the guitar along with the orginal
1. Play and sing together
1. Finally make it my own

  Chords: C C/E Em Em7 Em6
