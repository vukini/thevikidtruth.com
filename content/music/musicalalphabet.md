# The Alphabet

Notice the symmetries. Seconds are Sevenths reversed. Thirds vs Sixths & Fourths vs Fifths. Learn all the alphabets.
The thirds will give you access to all chord theory. The fourths and fifths to progressions. The others to extensions.

- A B C D E F G A B C D E F G A (Seconds)
- A C E G B D F A C E G B D F A (Thirds)
- A D G C F B E A D G C F B E A (Fourths
- A E B F C G D A E B F C G A E (Fifths)
- A F D B G E C A F D B G E C A (Sixths)
- A G F E D C B A G F E D C B A (Sevenths)
