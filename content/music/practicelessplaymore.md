#### Practice Less, Play More

You will do well to go to [https://www.playmoresongs.com](https://www.playmoresongs.com) or Amazon to purchase the book. Here are some of my notes taken
about the system:

1. Play to Music, forget metronomes, etc...
1. Forget Perfection
1. Bring the Music to your skill proficiency, not you to the music.
1. Program new habits into your life
1. - Match the Guitar to match your life, not your life to the guitar
    - Practice everyday using the
      [minimum effective dose (Arthur Jones)](https://gizmodo.com/4-hour-body-the-principle-of-the-minimum-effective-do-5709902)
    - 5 mins a day is enough.
    - Try the same time everyday. Then after some time, build up more time. Which is a total option.
1. Create games you are guaranteed to win
1. Prepractice
1. - Get the lyrics, figure out the key and chords in the song.
1. Practice
1. - Start Practicing the Songs you know well as a warm up.
1. Fast Practice
