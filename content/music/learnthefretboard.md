# Learning the Fretboard

## The Jazz Method for Learning the Fretboard

1. Know the Major Scale
1. Learn String by String
1. Play a chord then improvise on a single string. (C chord for example)
1. Then do with 2 strings at a time
1. Then change to simple other keys
1. Improvise blues on one string
1. Play tunes on one string
1. Then do on two adjacent strings
1. Then two non adjacent strings
