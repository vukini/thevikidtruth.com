#### Backing Tracks

- [QuistTV](https://www.youtube.com/c/QuistTV)

#### Caged Chord Practice

1. Do Caged Up and Down the Neck by Major and Minor Chord
1. Then by progression (for e.g. I IV V) palandromically
1. Then add lead notes between chord changes

# Practice Principles

- Consistency
- Program the action, statistically, mechanically vs artistically
- Progression
- Input Fuel
- The Background / Environment
- Incremental / Radical
- Antifragile, Stats
- Thinking, Awareness, Conciousness
- Patterns
- Tricks, Tips, Techniques
