# Swahili: Greetings and Essentials

Swahili 101: the first words to learn, grouped so they're easy to practise together. Every word is in the full list on [Swahili: Vocabulary](swvocab001.md).

## Greetings

Greetings matter in East Africa. Always greet before you ask anything.

| Swahili | English |
|---|---|
| **Habari gani?** | How are you? (what news?) |
| **Nzuri.** | Good, fine. |
| **Habari za asubuhi?** | Good morning (news of the morning?) |
| **Salama.** | Peaceful, fine. |
| **Hujambo?** – **Sijambo.** | How are you? – I'm fine. |
| **Hamjambo?** – **Hatujambo.** | How are you all? – We're fine. |
| **Hawajambo?** – **Hawajambo.** | Are they well? – They're fine. |
| **Shikamoo.** – **Marahaba.** | Respectful greeting to an elder – the elder's reply. |
| **Karibu.** | Welcome. |
| **Nimefurahi kukuona.** | I'm glad to see / meet you. |
| **Kwaheri.** | Goodbye. |

## Who's who: pronouns

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **mimi** I | **sisi** we |
| 2nd | **wewe** you | **nyinyi** you all |
| 3rd | **yeye** he, she | **wao** they |

- *Mimi ni mwanafunzi.* I am a student.
- *Wao ni marafiki zangu.* They are my friends.

## My, your, his/her

The ending changes with the noun class. With *jina* (name): **langu** my, **lako** your, **lake** his/her. With *nyumba* (house): **yangu**, **yako**, **yake**.

- *Jina langu ni VikiD.* My name is VikiD.
- *Jina lako nani?* What is your name?
- *Nyumba yangu iko Dubai.* My house is in Dubai.

## This and that

| Class | this | that | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| people (m-/wa-) | huyu | yule | *Huyu ni nani?* Who is this? |
| ki-/vi- | hiki | kile | *kitabu hiki*, this book |
| ji-/ma- | hili | lile | *jina hili*, this name |
| m-/mi- | huu | ule | *mti huu*, this tree |
| n- | hii | ile | *nyumba hii*, this house |
| place | hapa (here) | pale / huko (there) | *Habari za hapa?* How are things here? |

## Little words that do a lot of work

**ni** is, **si** is not, **na** and/with, **pia** also, **tu** only, **sana** very, **kidogo** a little, **sasa** now.

- *Ninasema Kiswahili kidogo tu.* I speak only a little Swahili.

## Numbers 1–10

moja, mbili, tatu, nne, tano, sita, saba, nane, tisa, kumi.
