# Esperanto: Vocabulary

My Esperanto word cards, gathered on one page. Each entry shows how the word is built (roots separated by ·), its meaning, and an example. For my long running list, see [Esperanto: Words I Am Learning](vortaro.md).

## Nature and animals

| Esperanto | Built from | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| bosko | bosk·o | grove, small wood | *Ni promenis tra la bosko.* We walked through the grove. |
| falko | falk·o | falcon | *La falko flugas alte.* The falcon flies high. |

## Describing

| Esperanto | Built from | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| svelta | svelt·a | slim, slender | *Ŝi estas alta kaj svelta.* She is tall and slender. The opposite is **dika** (fat, thick), not *malsvelta*. |

## Little words

| Esperanto | Built from | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| jam | | already, yet, by now | *Mi jam manĝis.* I have already eaten. Memory hook: **"I have already eaten too much yam!"** *Ĉu vi jam finis?* Have you finished yet? |
| per | | with, by means of, through | *Mi skribas per krajono.* I write with a pencil. |
| senpere | sen·per·e (without + by means of + adverb) | directly, without an intermediary | *Mi parolis senpere kun la direktoro.* I spoke directly with the director. |

## Going places

| Esperanto | Built from | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| deiri | de·ir·i (from + go) | to set out from, to depart | *Ni deiris de Dubajo matene.* We set out from Dubai in the morning. |
| deirpunkto | de·ir·punkt·o | starting point (of a journey) | *La stacidomo estas nia deirpunkto.* The station is our starting point. |
| gufujo | guf·uj·o (chill-out + container) | chill-out room (at a congress) | *Post la koncerto ni iris al la gufujo por trinki teon.* After the concert we went to the chill-out room to drink tea. |

The quote card *Granda telero, malplena kulero* is now on [Esperanto: Quotes](citiloj.md).
