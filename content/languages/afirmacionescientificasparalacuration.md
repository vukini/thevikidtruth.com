REVIEW OF THIS WORK

When Paramahansa Yogananda presented for the first time, more than eighty years ago, the principles and techniques set out in Scientific Affirmations for Healing, it would still be decades before the discoveries that have evidenced the connection between the body and the mind in the healing process.

Throughout those years, this pioneering work has taught hundreds of thousands of readers the basic knowledge that allows to reach and directly apply the extraordinary healing powers hidden in every human being.

Such knowledge is now making its way into traditional medicine thanks to the convergent vision of physics, psychology, neuroscience and spirituality
