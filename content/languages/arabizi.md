# Arabic: The Arabizi Alphabet

Arabizi writes Arabic with Latin letters, using **numbers for the sounds English doesn't have**. Each digit was chosen because it looks like the Arabic letter (3 looks like a mirrored ع). This page gathers my old one-letter cards. For the background, see [Arabic: Start Here](arabic.md).

| Arabizi | Arabic letter | Name | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | ء | hamza | glottal stop, the catch in "uh-oh" | *su2al* (سؤال) question |
| 3 | ع | ʿayn | a voiced squeeze deep in the throat | *3arabi* (عربي) Arabic |
| 3' or gh | غ | ghayn | gargled *gh*, like a French *r* | *ghali* (غالي) expensive |
| 5 or kh | خ | khāʾ | *ch* of Scottish "loch" | *5alas* (خلاص) enough, done |
| 6 | ط | ṭāʾ | emphatic, heavy *t* | *6ayyib* (طيب) good, OK |
| 6' | ظ | ẓāʾ | emphatic *dh* / *z* | *6'uhr* (ظهر) noon |
| 7 | ح | ḥāʾ | breathy *h* from the throat | *7abibi* (حبيبي) my dear |
| 8 | ق | qāf | *k* pronounced far back | *8alb* (قلب) heart |
| 9 | ص | ṣād | emphatic, heavy *s* | *9aba7* (صباح) morning |

Some regions use 9 for qāf and 8 for nothing at all, so expect variation. *ma3a salama* (مع السلامة) is "goodbye".
