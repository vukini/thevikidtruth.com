**the common currents! - the run of the mill!**

corriente - ordinary, common, garden variety 

 	Es un reloj corriente.

 	It's a common watch.

corriente -  electricity or water 

 	Se ha ido la corriente y no hay luz.

 	The electricity's gone so there's no light.

 	La barca fue arrastrada por la corriente.

 	The boat was dragged away by the current.
