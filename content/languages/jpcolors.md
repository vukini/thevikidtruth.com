# Japanese: Colours

My colours chart, with the basic colour words below. The four oldest colours (red, blue, white, black) are also **-i adjectives** and go straight before a noun: *akai kuruma* (赤い車), a red car.

![jocolors](/assets/jocolors.jpg)

| Japanese | Romaji | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 赤 / 赤い | aka / akai | red | *akai ringo* a red apple |
| 青 / 青い | ao / aoi | blue (traditionally also green) | *aoi sora* the blue sky |
| 白 / 白い | shiro / shiroi | white | *shiroi neko* a white cat |
| 黒 / 黒い | kuro / kuroi | black | *kuroi kutsu* black shoes |
| 黄色 / 黄色い | kiiro / kiiroi | yellow | *kiiroi hana* a yellow flower |
| 茶色 | chairo | brown (lit. "tea colour") | *chairo no kaban* a brown bag |
| 緑 | midori | green | *midori no ha* green leaves |
| 紫 | murasaki | purple | *murasaki no fuku* purple clothes |
| ピンク | pinku | pink | *pinku no T-shatsu* a pink T-shirt |
| オレンジ | orenji | orange | *orenji no bōshi* an orange hat |
| 灰色 | haiiro | grey (lit. "ash colour") | *haiiro no kumo* grey clouds |

Colours that aren't -i adjectives take **no**: *midori no ha*.
