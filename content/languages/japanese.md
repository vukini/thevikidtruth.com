## 1. Writing systems and pronunciation

Japanese mixes three scripts, often in one sentence:

| Script | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hiragana** (ひらがな) | grammar endings, native words | ありがとう *arigatō* thank you |
| **Katakana** (カタカナ) | foreign words, names, emphasis | コーヒー *kōhī* coffee |
| **Kanji** (漢字) | word roots borrowed from Chinese | 日本 *Nihon* Japan |

Each kana is one syllable: a vowel (**a i u e o**, said as in Spanish) or a consonant + vowel (**ka ki ku ke ko**). A long vowel (ō, ū) is held twice as long, and that changes meaning: *obasan* (aunt) vs *obāsan* (grandmother). Pitch is fairly flat; don't stress syllables the English way.

## 2. Core grammar

- **Word order is subject–object–verb**, and the verb comes last: *Watashi wa kōhī o nomimasu.* (私はコーヒーを飲みます) I drink coffee.
- **Particles** mark each word's role: **wa** (topic), **ga** (subject), **o** (object), **ni** (to, at), **de** (at, by means of), **no** (of, 's), **ka** (question).
- **No articles, no plurals, no gender**: *hon* is "a book", "the book" or "books".
- **Polite verbs end in -masu**: *tabemasu* (eat), *tabemasen* (don't eat), *tabemashita* (ate).
- **"X is Y"**: *X wa Y desu.* *Watashi wa VikiD desu.* I am VikiD.
- **Questions**: add **ka** at the end: *Nihongo o hanashimasu ka?* Do you speak Japanese?

## 3. Essential phrases

| Japanese | Romaji | English |
|---|---|---|
| こんにちは | konnichiwa | Hello |
| おはようございます | ohayō gozaimasu | Good morning |
| こんばんは | konbanwa | Good evening |
| はじめまして | hajimemashite | Nice to meet you (first meeting) |
| 私は ... です | watashi wa ... desu | I am ... |
| ありがとうございます | arigatō gozaimasu | Thank you |
| すみません | sumimasen | Excuse me, sorry |
| はい / いいえ | hai / iie | Yes / No |
| わかりません | wakarimasen | I don't understand |
| ... をください | ... o kudasai | ... please (asking for something) |
| さようなら | sayōnara | Goodbye |
