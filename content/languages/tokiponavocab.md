# Toki Pona: Vocabulary

Every Toki Pona word from my old one-word cards, gathered on one page and grouped by theme. Word classes: **n.** noun, **mod.** modifier (adjective/adverb), **vi./vt.** intransitive/transitive verb. One word can do all of these jobs: *moku* is "food" and "to eat".

## Living things

### jan

![jan](/assets/jan.png)

- n. person, people, human, being, somebody, anybody
- mod. human, somebody's, personal, of people
- vt. personify, humanize, personalize

### soweli

![soweli](/assets/soweli.jpg)

- animal, especially land mammal, lovable animal

### waso

![waso](/assets/waso.jpg)

- bird, winged animal

### kala

![kala](/assets/kala.jpg)

- fish, sea creature

### akesi

![akesi](/assets/akesi.png)

- n. non-cute animal, reptile, amphibian
- n. creeping animal, a scaly or slimy animal that creeps on land
- n. reptile, any air-breathing cold-blooded animal that crawls on land and has scales and bones; reptile
- n. amphibian, any cold-blooded animal with bones that lives on both land and in water; amphibian
- n. large arthropod, a large arthropod that lives on land; scorpion

### pipi

![pipi](/assets/pipi.jpg)

- bug, insect, spider

## Plants and food

### kasi

![kasi](/assets/kasi.jpg)

- plant, leaf, herb, tree, wood

### kili

![kili](/assets/kili.jpg)

- fruit, pulpy vegetable, mushroom

### pan

![pan](/assets/pan.jpg)

- grain, cereal

## Doing and making

### alasa

![alasa](/assets/alasa.jpg)

- vt. to gather, to collect food, resources or material needed for daily life and survival; to gather, harvest
- vt. to hunt, to pursue and kill animals to use as food and clothing; to hunt

### musi

![musi](/assets/musi.jpg)

- n. fun, playing, game, recreation, art, entertainment
- mod. artful, fun, recreational
- vi. play, have fun
- vt. amuse, entertain

### sitelen

![sitelen](/assets/sitelen.jpg)

- n. picture, image
- vt. draw, write
- n. representation, a visual or tactile work that serves to show, describe, explain or remind us of something else; representation, model
- n. picture, specific lines and shapes marked on a surface; drawing, print, painting, image, sign, sketch, outline, blueprint, etching, picture,
- n. diagram, chart, graph
- n. sculpture , an object made into the shape of something; carving, sculpture, figurine, replica

## Senses, mind and feelings

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **lukin** | n. eye, sight · vt. see, look at, watch, read · pre-verb: try to (*mi lukin sona*, I try to learn) |
| **oko** | n. eye (an older synonym of *lukin* in the official book) |
| **kute** | n. ear · vt. hear, listen; obey |
| **sona** | n. knowledge, wisdom · vt. know, understand; know how to (*mi sona toki*, I know how to talk) |
| **pilin** | n. heart, feeling, emotion · vt. feel, think, sense, touch |
| **olin** | n. love, compassion, affection · vt. love, respect (a person or animal) |
| **nasa** | mod. unusual, strange, silly, crazy, drunk |

## Life, food and talk

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **moku** | n. food, meal · vt. eat, drink, consume |
| **toki** | n. language, talking, speech · vt./vi. talk, say, speak · interj. hello! |
| **moli** | n. death · vi. die, be dead · vt. kill |
| **unpa** | n. sex, sexuality · vt./vi. have sex with |

## Examples

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *soweli li moku e kili.* | The animal eats fruit. |
| *mi lukin e waso.* | I see a bird. |
| *jan pona mi li olin e musi.* | My friend loves games. |
| *mi sona ala.* | I don't know. |
| *sina toki pona!* | You speak well / You speak Toki Pona! |
