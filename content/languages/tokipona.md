# Toki Pona

You can find the Official Website [here](http://tokipona.org/).

The language was developed by **Sonja Lang** as an experiment to understand how meaning
can be developed in 120 words only!

toki pona is part of a very rich tradition and community called the 'Conlangers'.
Conlang is short for **constructed language** and is an interesting idea in itself.

In the following page I will document my adventures in the land of Toki Pona.

Here is a nice [introduction video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pl_jeMu9-ko).

#### Tutorial

- **Noun + li + Noun** - something is something
- ```
   ijo - thing, jan - person, kili - fruit, lipu - document,
   meli - female, ni - this, soweli - animal
   ijo li ijo
   ni li jan
   ni li kili
   lipu li ijo
   jan li meli
   soweli li ijo
   meli li jan
   ni li lipu
   jan li jan
   soweli li meli
   soweli li ijo
   ni li lipu
  ```
- **Noun + li + Adjective** - Something is Someway
- ```
   telo - liquid, tomo - house, lili - little, pona - good, suli - big
   ijo li pona
  tomo li suli
  tomo li lili
  ni li telo
  telo li ijo
  tomo li ijo
  ```
- **Noun + Adjective** - Describe the Noun
- ```
   tomo suli
   jan pona
   meli lili
   telo suli
  ```
- **Noun + Noun2** - Use Noun2 as Adjective
- ```
   lipu soweli
   tomo meli
  ```
- **More Examples**
- ```
   telo li pona
   telo kili li suli
   meli li lili
   soweli lili li pona
   jan ni li suli
   soweli meli ni li pona
   jan ni li pona
   tomo telo li lili
   ni li soweli lili
   jan lili li meli
   lipu suli li pona
   kili li kili telo
  ```
- **You and I** - *li is not needed with mi or sina*
- ```
   mi - I, mije - man, sina - you, kulupu - community, sin - new, wawa - strong
   mi meje
   sina sin
   tomo mi
   kulupu sina
   tomo sina li sin
   mije li jan pona
   tomo soweli li lili
   sina mije wawa
   kulupu sin li wawa
   ni li lipu sina
  ```
- **Noun1 + li + Verb + e + Noun2** - Noun1 does verb action to Noun2
- ```
  jo - to have, kute - to listen, moku - to eat, pali - to make/do, sona - to know, toki - to talk
  ijo li pali e ijo
  mi moku e telo
  mije li sona
  mije li sona e ijo
  mi sona e toki pona
  mije ni li jan toki
  soweli suli li moku e sina
  lipu kulupu li wawa
  sina pali e moku sin
  jan sona li kute
  tomo sona li jo e lipu
  mi kute e sina
  kili li jo e telo
  kulupu li pali e tomo moku
  jan pali li jo e sona lili
  jan pona li moku e soweli
  meli li kute e meji
  kulupu lili li jo e toki sin.
  ```
- **More Adjective**
- ```
  ala - not, ike - bad, mute - many, pu - interacting with official toki pona book, sewi - sacred, wan - one/united
  ```
