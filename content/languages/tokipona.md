# Toki Pona: Start Here

**Practise:** [Toki Pona flashcards and quizzes](tokipona-practice.md) (works on any phone).


![tokipona](/assets/tokipona.png)

You can find the official website [here](http://tokipona.org/).

The language was developed by **Sonja Lang** (first published online in 2001, the official book *Toki Pona: The Language of Good* in 2014) as an experiment to see how much meaning can be built from around 120 words only!

Toki Pona is part of a very rich tradition and community called the "conlangers". Conlang is short for **constructed language**, and is an interesting idea in itself. On these pages I document my adventures in the land of Toki Pona.

## 1. Sounds and spelling

Toki Pona has just 14 letters, all lowercase except for proper names:

- vowels **a e i o u** (as in Spanish: *a* in "father", *e* in "bet", *i* in "machine", *o* in "go", *u* in "food")
- consonants **j k l m n p s t w** (*j* sounds like English *y*)

Stress is always on the first syllable: **TO**-ki **PO**-na. Names are adapted into these sounds and capitalised as adjectives: *jan Mewi* (a person called Mary), *ma Kanata* (Canada).

## 2. Core grammar

**Noun + li + noun**: something is something.

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *ni li jan.* | This is a person. |
| *lipu li ijo.* | A document is a thing. |
| *jan li meli.* | The person is a woman. |

**Noun + li + adjective**: something is some way. There is no word for "is".

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *ijo li pona.* | The thing is good. |
| *tomo li suli.* | The house is big. |

**Noun + adjective**: describe the noun (modifiers come after). A second noun can act as an adjective.

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *tomo suli* | big house |
| *jan pona* | good person, friend |
| *telo kili* | fruit juice ("fruit liquid") |
| *tomo telo* | bathroom ("water house") |

**You and I**: *li* is dropped after *mi* and *sina*.

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *mi mije.* | I am a man. |
| *sina sin.* | You are new. |
| *tomo sina li sin.* | Your house is new. |
| *kulupu sin li wawa.* | The new community is strong. |

**Noun1 + li + verb + e + noun2**: *e* marks the object.

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *mi moku e telo.* | I drink water. |
| *mi sona e toki pona.* | I know Toki Pona. |
| *soweli suli li moku e sina.* | The big animal eats you. |
| *tomo sona li jo e lipu.* | The school ("knowledge house") has books. |
| *meli li kute e mije.* | The woman listens to the man. |
| *kulupu lili li jo e toki sin.* | The small group has a new language. |

**Negation**: put *ala* after the word: *mi sona ala*, I don't know.

## 3. Essential phrases

| toki pona | English |
|---|---|
| *toki!* | Hello! |
| *mi jan VikiD.* | I am VikiD. |
| *sina pilin seme?* | How do you feel? |
| *mi pilin pona.* | I feel good. |
| *mi sona ala.* | I don't know. |
| *pona!* | Thanks! / Great! |
| *tawa pona!* | Goodbye (to someone leaving: "good going") |
| *awen pona!* | Goodbye (to someone staying: "stay well") |
