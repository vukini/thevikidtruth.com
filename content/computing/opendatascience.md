# Open Source Data Science

### The plain idea

Data science is the craft of turning raw data into understanding: collecting it, cleaning it, exploring it with statistics and visualisation, and often building models that predict or classify. "Open" data science means doing all of this with free, open-source tools and openly shared curricula, so anyone can learn it without expensive software or courses. This page points at exactly that, the Open Source Data Science Masters being a free, self-directed curriculum.

### Why it matters

Data is everywhere, and the ability to ask sensible questions of it, and to spot when an answer is nonsense, is one of the most valuable skills of the age. The open-source angle matters because it keeps that power widely available: the same tools used at the biggest companies (Python, R, Jupyter) are free for a student with a laptop. You are never locked out by a licence fee.

### An everyday analogy

Data science is detective work. The data is a pile of clues; statistics and visualisation are the magnifying glass and the corkboard with string between the photos; a model is the detective's theory of whodunit. And like a good detective, the skill is less in the gadgets than in asking the right questions and not jumping to conclusions.

### A 2026 note

The foundations in the linked curriculum, statistics, Python, SQL, visualisation, are evergreen, but the field has moved fast. The modern stack centres on **Python** (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn), **Jupyter** notebooks, and increasingly on **machine learning and large language models**. Tools like **Polars** (a faster pandas), cloud notebooks, and open models you can run yourself have joined the kit. The durable advice remains: learn the statistics first, the tools second, because a fancy model on misunderstood data is worse than no model at all.

### How it connects

- [Machine Learning](machinelearning.md): the modelling side of data science.
- [Python](python.md) and [Python Libraries](pythonlibrariestoolsandlinks.md): the dominant toolkit.

The truth revealed: data science is detective work with a laptop, and the open-source movement means the magnifying glass is free, the only scarce ingredient is the habit of asking careful questions.
