# On Becoming a Guru

There are several kinds of [Guru](guru.md) out there but the one I have always had in mind is to be something like
the great [renaissance masters](renaissancemasters.md), where by a computer is no longer just a tool but rather **an extension of my
soul.**

I have been of and on this course for many years but now I feel it is time to accelerate progress. There are a few
things going for us.

1. **There are many Guru's out there already** who are willing to help, **we truly are in the era of plenty**
1. Online **reading materials are plenty**
1. The number of tools out there are staggering, **the logical & physical are getting intertwined.**

Here is my proposed route (order is irrelevant):

- [BCIYH - Build a Computer in Your Head](bciyh.md)
- Hardware
- Os's
- Abstractions
- Languages
- Standards
- Tools
- Creating
