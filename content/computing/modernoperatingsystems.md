# Modern Operating Systems

## by Andrew S. Tanenbaum

![mos](/assets/mos.jpg)
{.right}

[Operating Systems](os.md) | [Books](books.md)

There are so many online resources to learn about OS's these days
but none come close to this magnificent book in terms of depth or quality.

What I really like about this book is that I feel it hits you (or me) with right level
of abstraction, especially for a superhero with no formal Computing education.

Vikid is self taught (but aren't we all?) and has a few superhero
friends on the internet to help him when needed.
