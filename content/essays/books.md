# Book Reviews & Notes

![reading-float-sat-600](/assets/reading-float-sat-600.jpg)
{.right}

viKid has a nasty habit of reading hundreds of books in parallel. The problem
with this approach is that he *never quite finishes anything and writing complete
reviews becomes difficult, or very slow at least.

**The solution?**

The concept of a blog bundled with a rolling release. As this is a
wiki, he can just
augment reviews as he reads more and more.

viKid currently owns an estimated 2000 books of which he has made in each a varying
amount of progress. As you can imagine, commenting and reviewing each of them is
probably a lifetime's work. He shall do his best now to get as far as he can. It
is hard to be a Superhero sometimes.
