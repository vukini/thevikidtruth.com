### About the book

*I Am a Strange Loop* by Douglas Hofstadter (2007) is the author's attempt to state, more directly than in his earlier *Godel, Escher, Bach*, what he believes the self actually is. His central claim is that consciousness and the sense of "I" arise from a *strange loop*: a self-referential pattern in which a system's symbols come to represent the system itself. The book weaves together logic, neuroscience and deeply personal reflection (including on the death of his wife) to argue that a self is a kind of pattern, or feedback loop, rather than a thing.
