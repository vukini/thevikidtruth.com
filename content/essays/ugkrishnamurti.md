# [UG Krishnamurti](ugkrishnamurti.md)

![UGKrishnamurti](/assets/ugkrishnamurti.jpg)
{.right}

This man was a total enigma. Here are some notes about
his life and 'teachings'.

- He had what can be called a kundalini awakening
- He lost interest in everything, his marriage failed, he became a pauper and roamed the streets of London
  for 3 years.
- He said all teachers have fooled themselves
- No mind --> there is only life, senses with connections, we are machines
- You don't have to do a thing, just let it happen, do not interfere, expect the unexpected
