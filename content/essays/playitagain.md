# Play It Again

## by Alan Rusbridger

### About the book

*Play It Again: An Amateur Against the Impossible* by Alan Rusbridger (2013) is a memoir. Rusbridger, then editor of *The Guardian*, sets himself the goal of learning to play Chopin's fiendishly difficult first Ballade in a year of just twenty minutes' daily practice, all while running a newspaper through the WikiLeaks and phone-hacking stories. The book interleaves the two struggles, making it both a meditation on amateur musicianship and a diary of a dramatic year in journalism.
