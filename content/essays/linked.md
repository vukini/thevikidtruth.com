# Linked by Albert-Laszlo Barabasi

### About the book

*Linked: The New Science of Networks* by Albert-Laszlo Barabasi (2002) is the popular book that brought network science to a wide audience. Barabasi, a physicist, explains how complex networks, the web, social ties, cells, the economy, share deep structural patterns, most famously the *scale-free* distribution in which a few richly connected "hubs" dominate. He introduces the "rich get richer" mechanism behind such hubs and shows why it explains everything from the robustness of the internet to the spread of epidemics. A foundational read for my [Networks](networks.md) notes.
