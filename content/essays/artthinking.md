# Art Thinking by Amy Whitaker

`Date: 06/10/2016`

I've been inspired to write by this rather unconventional book that try's to bridge
the world of art with that of business.

It does this by developing a new framework for both thought and action called
"Art Thinking", which is somewhat similar to the "Design Thinking" without being
retarded. Well at least thats how I like to think of it!

The book is written in a style that is easy to read and yet maintains enough depth
and complexity not to damage your sensibilities like most business books that read
more like self-help books.

I can recommend this book to anyone who wants to make thier life not just a roving
success, but a piece of work too!

Enjoy it and good luck :-)
