# Environments Anchor

Our Minds are oriented by the artifacts around us. Our understanding
    of **time**, **people**, **our beliefs**, **habits**, etc... are a function of what we
    place around us and where we situate our selves. This is because our
    physical surroundings **anchor** our psychological environments.  People
    who live in **fast** cities become **fast**. People who work in **organised**
    work environments become more **organised** themselves. People who live in
    **clean** environments, keep their inner environment **clean**. Etc...
