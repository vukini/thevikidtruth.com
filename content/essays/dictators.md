# The Dictators Learning Curve

##### Inside the global battle for democracy

`27/06/2016`

This is a book about the interplay between authoritarian regimes and the forces
that oppose them. It is about the historical developments in the politics and
implementation of such regimes.

As the title suggests, there is a 'learning curve' at work here, an evolutionary
arms race. There is a 'war' between the forces of democracy and authority.

Writing now, just days after the UK referendum ended in a Brexit, I can see
these forces at play, yet again.
