# Signals by Pipa Malmgrem

`Date: 04/12/2016`

## Notes

- **Erich Fromm**, writing in 1942, explained how the psychology of being a **‘senseless cog’** proved fertile soil for
    the **rise of Fascism in Germany, Italy and elsewhere in Europe.**
- Or as Jack Welch, former CEO of General Electric, put it: **'Control your destiny or someone else will!'**
- My central **purpose in writing this book is to give people the ability to notice signals** and thereby proceed
    into the future with a greater sense of the context that will influence any individual choices that we might make.
- Whenever the **majority of the population seeks to pursue the same idea at the same time, it usually ends in tears.**
