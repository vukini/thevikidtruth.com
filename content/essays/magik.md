# Magik by Dr. Lawrence Meredith

![magik](/assets/magik.jpg)
{.center}

` Date:  4th Dec 2016`

I bought this book in New York City, somewhere between Williamsburg
and Brooklyn on a particularly magical day. The birds were chirping, the air
was cool and the Sun was glowing most magnificently.

This book is something special and it's taken me more than a year to just
pick it up.

## Some Notes

- Alchemists, Magicians, and mystics all know that the mind does not flow
    from the brain. The know that the brain receives the mind
- Magik is a conscious process of deconstructing the strict
     Newtonian and Piagetian spaciotemperal causal
     world. It is like being put back into the embryonic state
      of homeostasis.... becoming a *babe in the abyss*.
- The dispersion of the soul is the idea that without
      the proper training, one will not maintain the proper
      psychical state of death, their central nervous system
      will become erratic, and their consciousness will disperse
      into oblivion
