# The Vikid Advaita

The mind is not just thoughts. Thoughts are the **Masculine** aspect of the Mind. Emotions are the **Feminine** aspect.

Notice you mind.

Thoughts create feeling, feeling creates thoughts. It is a dance.

What **drives** you in life. Plans, goals, desires, addictions, habits, etc... are these also mental objects?

Depression is a **lack of motivation**.

Beliefs. Beliefs about 'me' is the **ego**.

Sensations from your 5 senses are dualistic.

Mental objects:

- Thoughts
- Emotions
- Drives
- Beliefs
- Senses - 5 mental objects

The mind is the dance of the mental objects. The no mind is what is, that notices this.

This must be investigated.

**If you want to realise the real. Investigate the unreal.**
