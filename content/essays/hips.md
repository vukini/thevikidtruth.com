# Hidden in Plain Sight by Andrew Thomas

I really like this series of books by Andrew Thomas, which I would really classify under
speculative science. Speculative as opposed to orthodox. I like to read such works because
I often find Orthodox Science (the kind of stuff that comes out of Universities) very
conservative. Sometimes you just need to read material that is not fully accepted by main
stream science. It keeps you very  much more focused and you are forced to really think
about what you believe. When reading someone like Brian Green or Laurence Krauss the
tendency is for the mind to just **accept** the **Truth** as it is handed down to you.
