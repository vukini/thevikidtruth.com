# Quantum Mechanics for Thinkers by Gennaro Auletta and Shang-Yung Wang

![qmthinkers](/assets/qmthinkers.jpg)
{.right}

This is a book on Quantum Mechanics that is aimed at what I call the **semi-lay** or **pro-am** reader.
A place that I put myself squarely into.

It is split into three parts:

1. [Superposition](superposition.md "MISSING PAGE"), [as Vectors](states.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Bases](bases.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Operations](operations.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Complementarity](complementarity.md "MISSING PAGE")
1. Formal Issues on [Position & Momentum](positionmomentum.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Energy and QM Dynamics](eqm.md "MISSING PAGE"),
     [Angular Momentum & Spin](aspin.md "MISSING PAGE")
1. Ontological Issues regarding [Measurement Problem](measurementproblem.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Non-Locality](nonlocality.md "MISSING PAGE"),
     [Non-Separability](nonseparability.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Quantum Information](qi.md "MISSING PAGE"), [Quantum Interpretation](qinterpretation.md "MISSING PAGE")
