# Why Information Grows by Cesar Hidalgo

### About the book

*Why Information Grows: The Evolution of Order, from Atoms to Economies* by Cesar Hidalgo (2015) argues that economic growth is best understood as the growth of *information*, physical order embodied in products and in the networks of people who know how to make them. Hidalgo, a physicist and complexity researcher, links thermodynamics, information theory and economics to explain why some regions prosper: they accumulate "knowhow" distributed across many minds. It connects to my notes on [knowledge representation](krep.md) and [networks](networks.md).
