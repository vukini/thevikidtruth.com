# Articles

I have always fancied myself a bit of a writer, ha ha :-) but I know I am not
one of those, I have read too many who are.
It doesn't matter too much anyway. What I do hope is that you like
what I have written. There will be a few mistakes, especially grammatical errors and
typos. I was never any good at all that. I have also changed my views significantly
since these articles were written. **So what is here is only what I was thinking when
I wrote it and not necessarily things I hold dear or near now.**
Recently, I have decided a policy that may be
summarized as **'publish whatever'**. That means I won't generally wait till I
have finished whatever I am working on before I publish. This is generally because
I don't actually finish anything I start! It is just the viKid way.
