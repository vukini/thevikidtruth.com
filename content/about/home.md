# The viKid Truth

###### A *Radical Approach* to Thought

Welcome to the personal site of **viKid**. I am a superhero with **the power to reveal
the Truth.**

This is a space where I will be exploring the various ideas and disciplines
that interest me, and hopefully you. The types of topics we will cover are wide
and varied but principally revolve around [music](music.md),
[math](math.md), [physics](physics.md), [computing](computing.md), [philosophy](philosophy.md),  [languages](languages.md), [politics](politics.md),
[business](business.md "MISSING PAGE"), [finance & economics](economics.md) and more.

I have always been inspired by the 'personal' website. Not many Superhero's have one.
Superman and Batman are wusses!

I think a personal website offers more than
what is achieved by blogging on a platform or making an action movie.
You get more control of your content
and the way you want to communicate with your audience *(movie producers are very
demanding and want you to perform too many stunts for my liking.)*

Saying that, I have been mightily inspired by the site of [Xah Lee](http://xahlee.org)
whos work continually delights me. I hope that one day, this wiki, will achieve a
 similar status, depth and breath of content. **What this site will achieve however is
Superhero stardom.**

Please note that this site is powered by [picolisp](picolisp.md), a wonderful little language
and virtual machine that I believe has a great future. It is built by another Superhero.
