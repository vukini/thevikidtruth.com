### Should kids spend more time outside with friends?

The first question we must ask is, which outside? Is it the same outside as 30, 40 or 50 years ago?

I would say kids are not stupid. If their outside is decent, that’s precisely where they would be. However, if their outside is all fenced off into private communities, full of chain-stores, is dangerous or exhibiting any other antisocial factor, they will have less incentive to go out.

What about hanging out with real friends? Do any of their actual friends live in walking distance? Can they cycle freely without being assaulted by large roads with speeding cars? Are there places that they can hang out without being hassled?

I can bet the answer in most cases would be no, otherwise they would already be outside.

On habit building. If you are a parent, I bet you are closer to what we call a **helicopter parent**. That means you are constantly supervising and hovering about your children. I bet they are never out of your sights (or some minders) for very long. And then after that, you give them a mobile phone so you can keep tabs on em’. Yeah… guess what they do with those phones. Most kids hate being supervised constantly and the won’t mature properly if you do.

So you boxed them in, put them in a shitty environment, monitor them constantly - and you want them to spend more time outside under those circumstances? Fat chance.
