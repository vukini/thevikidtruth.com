There’s a guitar teacher out there that everyone should know, whether you play the instrument or not. His name is [Michael Palmisano.](https://www.guitargate.com/)

He started out by mixing Guitar Instruction with Reaction videos. *Ultra Creative Magic.* Today, he does a lot of entrepreneurial stuff. He has a website with a large community of music fans, he teaches others how to build online businesses as well, he discusses culture and other subjects. I’ve become a lifetime member of his club (@guitargate), it’s not very expensive for what you get. *Damn good value for money in fact.*

It’s my recommendation that you subscribe to Michael’s Channel. Yes there is guitar stuff, but more than that, you will learn about *Music*. You will learn how songs are put together, what artistry is, how to leverage talent, appreciate nuances that would have ordinarily never occurred to you, how music videos work, etc etc. You will start to hear thing and know what needs to be done to create them.

That’s magic right there…
