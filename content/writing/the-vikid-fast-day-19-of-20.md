## Breakfast

Tomorrow at 10am, I’ve done 20 days, though **today has been far the hardest.** It’s all in the mind, just the sight of the finish line means my stomach is already operating, I can feel the acid building up.

All I can say, that beyond conventional wisdom, *fasting is an amazing experience* and doable by any normally functioning human being. You do not have to eat for 20 days, you need no supplements, you probably don’t need the coconut water either, but based on my lifestyle and my intimate relationship with the fruit, it was a lovely edition and faithful friend.  
You can continue with a fairly normal life and even take an adventure or two. You will function, not just adequately but with a love and appreciation of life. I wholeheartedly recommend it. **Drop convention and take the plunge.**

At 10am, I break fast. *There’s a sensible way to do it but I’m not sure how sensible I feel!*

Look forward to seeing you all there for another celebration, for now I will enjoy the sunset…
