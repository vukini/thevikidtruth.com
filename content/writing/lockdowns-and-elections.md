It’s been over a month since I’ve written. I avoided all news quite literally and didn’t follow the US election, as the results were utterly predictable:

- **Total chaos with all the usual suspects playing their parts exactly as required with Oscar winning performances.**

- **A polarized populace playing team sports without any regard for the truth.** “As long as we win, anything is acceptable…”

- **Democrazy delegated to the Judiciary.** Further undermining faith in the law and putting judges under enormous political pressure.

- **Meme culture, rampant.** Reminds me of all the jokes going around before the first lock down.

Not many people are laughing anymore.

It may sound defeatist but I actually spend a considerable amount of time with the *highly educated and commercially successful.* I have found them to be equally ignorant as the “lesser” population. (Lesser in their eyes)

**The only difference is that they more sure of themselves.** You see, when you think that you are smarter than average (with a certificate to prove it) and have a good job (with a house & bank balance to show for it), you tend to believe in your own BS with much greater voracity.

So I’ve given up, trying to convince anyone of anything, one might as well speak to a brick wall. No, sorry, speaking to a brick wall is more beneficial! At least you won’t lose friends if you speak to the wall and will learn something about yourself.

I’ve given up trying to convince myself of anything while I am at it. It took me a while (a good 40 years) to realize that I was really just as dumb (actually dumber) as anyone else. That I too had an ego the size of Jupiter and that I spent my entire youth batting for the forces of the Idiocracy. So I have really no complaint against others. It’s just how the world works.

It required copious levels of destruction of my financial wealth and mental health to see through the problem and that is why I thank the universe for all the knocks it has given me. Without them, I would be blissfully blind. Not to say that I am not still blind but experience is a wonderful teacher. **Now, I am Blissful in Blindness.**

So while we all look the other way, the world will go into another lockdown.
