Since the world is essentially infinite and all representations finite, representations need to be by definition, “compressions” or “symbols” of the original “reality”.

Interpretation is the reverse process, the decompression of data. The following of symbols into mental presentations.

When I write "Pink Elephant," the mind must follow the words, recreating the symbol, regenerating the words into pictures, sounds and feelings in your mind.
