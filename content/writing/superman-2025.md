Now Vikid had some rather interesting habits. If habits is what you call them. One such activity was the pursuit of Psychonautics. The altering of consciousness by technologies ancient and modern, through the use specialized techniques not known to many.

And so it happened that **Superman** had recently made a movie about himself. Vikid, being the *Hero of Truth*, had yet to star in a film but scoping the competition was another favorite hobby of his.

So he headed to the theatre one afternoon.

Last minute, he had an idea to watch the movie in an altered state. Superman could fly, but Vikid could fly too… noeticly.

And what a wonderfully fun movie it was, a surround sound, *Punk Rock*, extravaganza.

That Superman, not only can he fly (faster than light), shoot lasers out his eyes, not only is he bulletproof, but he can make movies as well!

The story is heartwarming, the acting is superb, the action, never ending, though there are enough moments to breathe and take it all in.

The color pallet is positively psychedelic,

And it’s got a cute dog.

What more can you ask for?
