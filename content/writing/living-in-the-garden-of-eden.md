It seems that my life is dream. All the joy, love and turmoil, a figment of the great imagination. It fills the universe, inside and out, it quenches consciousness with a gorgeous light.

Life has been me with a guitar or mandolin in hand. Sitting in parks, under magnificent trees across glimmering lakes with old souls and young cheeky hearts.

Life has been listening to classical music at the Carnegie Hall with loved ones. Ethereal tones that transcend space, a hundred violins singing in concert, a thunderous drum. A harp plucking the air itself apart, harmonics in the quantum…

*A Song to Peace*.

Life has been visiting an old friend in California. A friend that has been with you since before time began. To visit the eternal peace, the fount of compassion and fountain of grace itself.

Life in this garden is not without its thorns. Rushing back home under the auspices of another World War on your doorstep.

But coming back and being greeted with all the joy of a long lost pup who finds its owner. A jumping hug and kisses makes up for any concern.

We are living in Eden. Blue skies, vast deep oceans, deserts and forests. Mountain tops, the wind and mystery all around us.

Listen to music that nourishes your inner being.

Life is to lived from the heart.

We only need to open our eyes (ears and soul) to see.

And that’s,
