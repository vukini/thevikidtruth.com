## Energy & Emanations

14 days have past without food and I feel better for it. Who would have thought?

*My energy is still great.* I was up at 3 am this morning. After trying to force myself to sleep unsuccessfully, I finally got out of bed at 4am. I broke another rule (what are rules for?) and watched two fabulous interviews on [ConsciousTV](https://www.youtube.com/user/conscioustv) and [Buddha at the Gas Pump](https://www.youtube.com/c/Batgap). Both are wonderful channels that explore spirituality with authenticity. Try them out!
