Animist.

This what I have become, for I believe that Spirit is behind every movement. Nothing moves without spirit. Or even better, Spirit is the movement.

If you slow down for a moment and simply follow your breath, you will find this is true.

I’ve been partying in Delhi the last couple of days. The place just rocks. It’s got a great vibe. And last night, some random Italian guy gave me the Sermon on the Mount in a noisy club.

It is as if Jesus him self had arisen. It just came out of the blue. I was just chillin and dancing. When this guy just came in and starting blasting something into my ear.

I had to do a double take for every word he said rang true. More than that it sounded profound. No sooner was the sermon given that he disappeared.

I continued to dance into the night because it is the spirit which moves.

And That’s,

### The Vikid Truth
