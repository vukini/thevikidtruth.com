**There are some benefits to waking up.** When you read (this includes, watch, listen and hear) as much as I have, I think it’s an inevitable side effect. When you’ve inhabited thousands of minds, you just see through the fog with laser like vision. It’s like being the one eyed man in the land of the blind. Awesome really.

And I think I have awoken. (The opposite of going “woke” incidentally). For I see a lot of awakened masters and realize they are still in infancy. It’s a bit of a hobby for me. I find some enlightened guru (usually with a book and an unhealthily large following), find them on zoom. Speak with them, sometimes for an hour, some times for weeks. Realize they are morons, then rinse and repeat. Reading book, whether it is on finance or the state of contemporary cricket is the same thing. Meeting a friend. Same thing.

But it’s also a bit of an oxymoron. If you understand anything about non-duality or follow any mystic religion deeply, you know that, **no one can awake** and ultimately you are just conversing with yourself. Those morons… they are you. You are the grand moron. That’s the universal joke!

Which is the same as saying,

> “everyone is awake”.

Kind of like saying ,

> *“if everyone is special, then no one is special…”*

but that’s a subject for another day.

However, there are some severe downsides too.

> *Like Noah, I see total catastrophe.*

Let me be clear here. 15 years ago, I was Great Thunberg’s alter ego. I saw global warming and mankind as a cancer.

**How wrong could I have been.**

> ps, shhhh… there’s no such thing and we ain’t no tumor.

Then there was a short period of calm and positivity as I shifted from one vision to the other.

**But now, catastrophe strikes again. And unlike Noah, this time there is no boat you can build. Preppers beware.**

I am fully aware that this may just be a state of mind. That the normal state of affairs will continue, that life as usual may continue. The *“new normal”* may actually just become normal. The sky is probably not falling, Zelensky is a super guy, wearing three masks, jabbing yourself every six months while waving a yellow and blue flag and eating bugs is a duty.

(Is that you? Be honest)

0h, and that microchip (coming soon!) on your arm and under your balls, a necessity.

But then I think again. The stories of prophets also exist. In all traditions, might I add.

Even science has that “Onestone” guy. Yeah, the light speed is constant, let’s build a nuclear bomb before the other side guy. How did that work out?

Theses prophets communicated with higher dimensions and came back to Earth. (*Yep, other dimensions exist, believe it or not*)

Often they took a long time to just believe in themselves. **Most of society ignored them (usual case), or if they were successful, either they were worshipped, burned at the stake or both.** The really successful ones could propheteer with a harem of young, beautiful and devoted students, if you catch my drift. It’s a dangerous but rewarding business!

So what does one do with visions, does one keep them to thyneself or does one propheteer?
