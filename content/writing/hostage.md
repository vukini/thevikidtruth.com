Certain dates get etched into our memories, Oct 7 is now one of them. It's the date Eli Sharabi was abducted and taken hostage by Hamas terrorists into Gaza.

This book documents Eli’s journey from that day till his negotiated release.

It's a story about survival under the most horrifying of circumstances. It's about how one can dig deep to keep their senses under extreme pressure. It's about true psychology, when all the theory is stripped of it's academic scaffolding.

Eli’s story is not inherently political. It's about an ordinary guy put in an extraordinary situation. Yes, there is grief, but there is also resilience and hope.

It doesn't matter which side of the political divide you are on. *First hand accounts such as this, so contemporary, are rare.* Furthermore, it takes an extraordinary soul to give such an honest account, with no frills, no melodrama.

*This story will teach you something profound…*
