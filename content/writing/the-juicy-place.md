“Just sitting with discomfort, without trying to escape or numb it in anyway, without exception, without a goal in mind, without seeking anything, including some abstract notion of “comfort” —that’s the juicy place, the place of creative transformation, the place where newness shines” — Jeff Foster, The Way of Rest

![](/assets/the-juicy-place/3f66035aab78.jpeg)

Advice that is so simple and yet so hard to follow. It seems to get at the core of everything.

It’s a direct affront to restlessness, to everything we are on a daily basis.

**To confront discomfort by embodying it.**

A few of years ago Jeff was hit with a rare and debilitating disease. It knocked him for a six and he had to eat his own words.
