Time to awaken from this dreamy sleep,

which has sometimes been happy and sometimes deep.

Deep into chasms of despair and dread.

Time to wake up sleepy head.

The Mind continually seeks,

perhaps I should stop resisting.

Dopamine hits, keeps hitting and hitting.

I cannot rest, this mind is so strong.

Wake up Sleepy head, Wake up, Wake up.
