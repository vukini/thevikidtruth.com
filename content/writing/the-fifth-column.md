I spent most of my childhood and quite a substantial part of being an adult not knowing about **auto-immune disease.** Take me for a fool, that much I can admit. I was an MTV grooving, Gameboy loving kind of kid. Never paid attention to this kind of thing.

*Then about 10 years ago I started noticing a trend,* this new thing called auto-immune disease. I know it isn’t new but that’s how it felt to me. And then the trend started getting larger. **Friends and family that I grew up with, acquaintances that I know started started getting it.** It manifests in many forms.

Maybe you have heard of:

- **Hashimoto’s** (Thyroid Problem)

- **Ciliacs** (Gluten Intolerance)

- **MS** (Central Nervous System)

- **Graves** (Thyroid Problem)

- Type 1 **Diabetes**

- **Lupus**

- Various forms of **Arthritis**

- **Myocarditis** (Heart)

The list goes on and on…

**Here are some of the common symptoms:**

- fatigue

- achy muscles

- swelling and redness

- Low grade fever

- trouble concentrating

- numbness and tingling in the hands and feet

- hair loss

- skin rashes

I suffer from 5 of the above… and there’s a good chance this is because I live my life like a jackass but it does make you think…

Then consider this:

> *The study included 14,211 participants, 12 years and older, in the U.S. National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES). The scientists used immunofluorescence, a technique that uses fluorescent dye to visualize antibodies, to examine the frequencies of ANAs in subjects from three time periods. They found that ANA prevalence for 1988-1991 was 11.0%, while for 1999-2004 it was 11.5%, and for 2011-2012 it was 15.9%. These percentages corresponded to 22, 27, and 41 million affected individuals, respectively.*
>
> [source: NIH, US Government](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/autoimmunity-may-be-rising-united-states)
