**Stop the espressos, meditate a little, do some exercise and eat well.**

It reminded him of a few months before the pandemic. Around September 2019, he started to feel really disturbed. A premonition of some kind perhaps. That feeling lasted a full three years, peaking sometime near the end 2022.

Watching nearly everyone around him and around the world lose themselves to fear. Being shouted at or scolded almost daily for not wearing a mask properly.

When he says “almost everyone” he means everyone bar maybe 3 adults he knew personally. *Three in millions.*

He remembers being made “the other” for refusing the jab. Unable to travel, even to a neighboring city for having a mobile-app that showed the color red instead of green.

Being looked at by others like a mad man.

Seeing the fear in everyone’s eyes and to *what extent people go to when they are in genuine fear.*

We as a society have collectively and conveniently forgotten this episode that was concluded in a single day.

He still remembered that day it was announced in the news paper the “Pandemic was over” and like a switch, all went back to normal.

It was like a computer game had reset itself and all the amnesiac NPC’s (Non Player Characters) just carried on as if nothing had happened for two and half years.

Talking of computer games, it was fun watching his children discover the gaming-console. Especially *driving games,* which were always his favorites.

It takes a little time to master the correct technique and counter instinct.

Vikid now watched this in real time with his children. He also watched how the human brain works things out by itself, how the corrections are made by trial and error. No major instructions need to be given.

Now the kids are better than him!
