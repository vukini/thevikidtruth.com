> I do not see with my eyes
>
> for all night I see in my dreams
>
> and during the day too my mind flies
>
> through imaginal realms

> Neither do I see with my mind
>
> it is a mere participant
>
> a subject,
>
> and object in the visual realm.

> I do not see with my brain.
>
> That lump of grey matter neither sees or hears anything.
>
> A clump of cells firing with electrical potential.
>
> That is not sight.

> I do not see
>
> but sight happens…
>
> Eyes open or closed it continues
>
> It appears out of Nothing.
