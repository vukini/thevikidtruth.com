### So What is a Lie?

Well it is an old adage to say,

> “The Map is not the Territory”.

By this we mean that any representation (now on called the “map”), is NOT the thing we are representing (the “territory”). **And this is necessarily so.** A picture of an apple is not an apple. The word “car” is not a car.

**1 + 1 does not equal 2**, contrary to popular belief.

*A map is a sort of guide.* To the extent that it is useful, the map is **good**. To the extent that it isn’t it is **bad**.

Notice here that each map needs a **User**. And the User must **interpret** the map within a territory. The user must also have an **intention**. He must use the map to **navigate** the **territory**.

What I mean by a lie can now be established.

**A lie, is a map, that the user believes to be good, that in reality, is not** and this can be for many reasons.

The lie may be malicious, perhaps you have been given a misleading map by an agent provocateur.

However today we will not consider this sort of Lie. **We will rather concern ourselves with maps which are unintentionally bad, that may be there due to historical reasons or due to hubris.** For these types of maps are not only more numerous, but they are far more dangerous and form the basis of our modern societies.
