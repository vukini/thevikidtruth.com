*And yet, these people never brought forth any discussion about his race.*

Vikid, did not live in the clouds, he was aware of a contingent of Race defined Nationalists. But they weren’t Conservatives with a big C. They were mathematically insignificant and politically irrelevant (for now). These people existed in the fringe of every country.

*Now, Vikid was unsure about “facts”.*

**Were we all seeing the same thing or just interpreting the same facts in different ways?**

He had understood that in Computer Science, all data had to be *interpreted*. The data “0101” could mean the number 5, a shade of grey or a signal to launch a rocket 🚀 in Ukraine, depending on what software system was reading it.

*The same data could mean anything.*

Neuroscience had shown us something further. *Eyes didn’t See.* Sight was constructed not reflected. The brain being a lump of mush capable of virtual reality.

Data, analogous to facts.

*Except facts on a human scale were orders of magnitude more complex than anything in silicon.*

A simple fact, *“It is raining”*, would probably require thousands lines of code if we were to match human capability. *Astonishing*.

So complicated that Vikid considered, any “fact” may be completely and solely *interpretation*.

**The facts themselves inconsequential. It’s as if they didn’t even exist.**

The simple fact, *“It is raining”*, could mean:

- *“Don’t go outside”* - a mother speaking to a child

- *“The harvest is dead”* - a farmer to his wife

- *“Get the umbrella”* - a boss giving orders to his secretary

**Context was everything.**

The vaccine debate (or lack of it) being another wonderful example of facts being ignored or *misinterpreted to the point of not being there at all.*

In fact he witnessed this daily.
