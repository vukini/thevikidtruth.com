It was a bright day in a dark room. That’s where Vikid, the Superhero of Truth, liked to reside while thinking of wonderous schemes. He had just defeated **Liar** by setting his pants on fire… a fun little story, [found at this link](https://open.substack.com/pub/thevikidtruth/p/liar-liar?r=73toq&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web).

This day he was pondering over the *Nature of Wealth* and how it related to the age old questions of *Mind & Matter.*

![](/assets/super-abundance/59563e1c1484.jpeg)

Along his wondering he had chanced upon a book called **“Superabundance”** , written by a Think-Tank Wonk, who claimed many a fantastical thing.

The Wonk wasn’t wrong but what he said had many implications worth considering.

> ***Wealth*** **was a product of** ***Thought over Matter,***
>
> **however people perceived it as** ***limited*****.**
>
> **They reasoned it was governed by material things.**

The Wonk was also hampered by a one dimensional unitary focus on Economics and Pollyanna-thinking.

However, this didn’t bother Vikid. The book wasn’t designed to be *The Only Answer.* It was another piece of the puzzle he wished more people would engage in.

**“What was an iPhone to a Cat or Farming to a Dog?”, he wondered.**

It made no sense except to *thinking beings.*

Now, physicists showed us that,

> matter = energy.
>
> E *equals* em cee squared i‘twas said.

The idea could be used to *generate power from an atom* for good or bad, or lie dormant as it had since the beginning.

*Sand rearranged as chips* *could calculate the weather* or sit inert in a desert, baking under the Sun forever.

The point being that, **there was no practical limit to wealth on the planet**. All we needed was imagination and cooperation.

> Mind *over* Matter
>
> NOT
>
> Matter *on the* Mind.
>
> Which was nothing other than
>
> *Craving.*

**We were not running out of Sand or Energy.**

> And that “Climate” discussion,
>
> Net *f’ing* Zero,
>
> an oldskool eschatological obsession.

**Exactly the opposite of common understanding.**

> Which spread from person to person
>
> from *Malthus to Marx,*
>
> from *Thunberg to Thanos,*
>
> *threatening half of life in existence.*

**There was no reason to fight as nothing was running out**…

except for our own patience and self-esteem.

**So what were we fighting about constantly and,**

**more importantly why?**

*Mind had a habit of crystallizing to matter.*

*Thought had a habit of reducing to belief…*

and the number of false beliefs vastly outnumbered the true.

This being the result of *the law of large numbers* and other statistical blunders.

Old thoughts about scarcity were rife in society, leading to **fear about availability.**

> *The power-hungry,*
>
> *always scared about losing control*
>
> *of ill gotten goods.*
>
> *The power-starved scared of losing*
>
> *what little they had and*
>
> *losing favor to the power-hungry.*

Each in turn trying to grab what they could, *by any means.* Slaves to each other and their own beliefs.

But it didn’t have to be that way.

*There could be plenty for all.*

**Not through some cock-n-bull theory of “Redistribution” or preoccupation about “Capitalism”.**

*But through creation.*

> *Mind creating Matter and*
>
> *Matter creating Mind*.
>
> *Mediated through non-aggression and liberty.*

**The trick was to see that the limits we placed, were put there by ourselves.** The horrors we created were created by us. The subservience to Power at the cost of dignity was a self inflicted wound. *Power was not to be feared and not to be hated.* Power could be enjoyed, shared and put to productive use without sacrificing love, culture and a deep respect for history.

*This wasn’t about singing Kumbaya or going Hippie.* Those were false prophecies.

It was about putting mind over matter through ***fearless study***. It was about facing the Demons of Society and beating them with *uncensored* *discussion* and *honesty*.

Vikid opened the curtains and let the light in. The room sparkled with promise.

*“Thanos, I’m coming to get you.”*, he whispered under his breath.

He smiled at the prospect. Until next time…

for this was,
