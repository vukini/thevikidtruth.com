It isn’t kosher to recommend or review a book without reading it. However, that’s what I am going to do.

Like being a scout at a local school trial, you just know when a kids got it. I had that same feeling when I first saw Alcaraz. That boy was going to be a phenomenal Tennis player. Sometimes you just know.

So it goes with this book.

![](/assets/the-book-scout-and-his-mystical-kingdom/eaa21e13f06d.jpeg)

So far, I’ve just read the first story, not even the entire chapter. It starts off with *Ian Taylor*, the founder of *Vitol*, the world’s largest commodity trader. It begins with a plane’s plunging descent into Benghazi, where a deal is made that changes history. I thought I was well informed. Guess not. Never heard about this guy, never understood how these things worked.

I would like to say more here but I don’t feel entirely free to (that’s where the experiment comes in). So, for now, I recommend you buy the book. *But you’re not a nutter like The Vikid Truth, so I doubt you will.*

![](/assets/the-book-scout-and-his-mystical-kingdom/0da930e2be9c.jpeg)

That’s what I bought in the last 2 days. I’m a book scout and this one is a winner.

### A Possible New Experiment

I’ve decided to go with intuition, it came to me as a spark this morning. The idea was to monetize part of this Substack.

It’s not for the money. Unlike Elon’s Twitter I am not hemorrhaging cash but like the Tweetie Bird, I want to restrict some of what I write to only the committed. *Commitment requires a sacrifice.* Even if it is tiny.

Let me explain.

The world is upside down, it really is. Freedom ain’t what it used to be, so we need a walled garden to play within.

I have only 50 subscribers, most of who are friends and family.

(***I’m not asking you for money & thank you for Subscribing*** )

This site is open access, so in theory anyone can peer in. However, there are things I want to write about that are not wholly appropriate for the general public and I don’t want it going out to everybody.

For example:

1.  A book I plan on writing. Once finished it will be made free. For the time being I will post chapters here.

&nbsp;

2.  Sensitive Commentary. Things that could be understood wrongly if read ‘out of context’.

3.  Other experimental writing. I have adventures that most people wouldn’t believe.

4.  Money matters.

5.  Spiritual Talk (not for the uninitiated).

6.  Audio and Video experiments.

7.  Experiments with music and code.

I would still like people to read my writing, it’s not a secret. I want it out there in the *ether*, not on a hard-drive rotting. Two books of mine died there already.

**However, by putting a paywall in front of it, only those chosen by interest or fate will enter the Mystical Kingdom of Vikid.**

Elon made a similar calculation, so I think I’m in worthy company.

**What do I promise for the small fee?**

I can’t make too many promises on an experiment. However, I will write a lot more freely and make it worthwhile, it maybe even enlightening. With freedom comes expressive power which I have been limiting.

As for magic, there’s going to be plenty of that.

All you have to do is tap in…
