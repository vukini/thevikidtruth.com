He wakes up every morning. Bones and muscles ache. A nebulous haze envelops everything. He is not sure what it is, some knowing from within, inaccessible to his thought matrix, the intuition that something’s awry. That feeling of being *out of phase* with everyone by that slightest of margins. *It is actually about 5 years.* A part of him seemed to live 5 years in the future. Not much in the span of a human lifespan, and a sliver of history.

Enough to crack the illusion but *not enough to take you out of it*.

An inhabitant of the Liminal space, stuck between the Nether world and the Akashic, thrown into the density known as Earth.

These kind of thoughts have been well known for centuries and it is an integrated part of our culture. Films like The Matrix depict it as an artificial control structure, a simulation that only a few escape with the help of a pill. TV shows like Stranger Things, explore ideas about other dimensions and what could be lurking inside them, requiring advanced science and machinery to tear a hole in the fabric of reality.

From ancient times we have groups such as the *Gnostics*, who believed that the world was created by the Demiurge as a way to bind our souls away from the *true reality*. The Gnostics have been around for at least 20 centuries.

*Hindu mythology has probably the largest and most in depth exploration of possible spaces and dimensions ever imagined.* It’s been around another 2 or 3 times longer than that. It is claimed and posited that many a Rishi (a seer or sage) have explored these spaces and come back to report their findings. Their access to these places being granted by extremes of meditation, asceticism and single pointed devotion.

**But it’s one thing to read about it and another to be wrapped in it.**

Vikid, being a *Superhero of Truth*, could vouch for them. For he too had been around some of these extraordinary realms. First taking access the same way Neo did, later by the way of the Rishis.

![](/assets/out-of-phase-702/1879c9d925dc.png)

Before we proceed, let’s not forget other manifestations of this particular kind of feeling. Generally less immersive, *like a belief in a creator that you have a personal relationship with.* A creator who tells you what to eat and what not. How to behave. A creator who guides you, who consoles you when you lose your job and helps you decide the best course of action when you get competing offers from the slavers of *Microsoft* and *Apple*.

Yes, many variations on that theme. A prayer for a good (and rich) husband. A prayer to the lord that your team wins a football game, that grandma makes it through her operation.

At the end of the day, *it’s the belief in something unseen yet felt at the core of your being*.

The plea, as a way of communicating to the realm of divinity.

A fruitful place of research and inquiry is the phenomenon of **Near Death Experiences,** NDE’s. Here access is granted the other realms by one’s nearness to crossing to the other side, actually dying for a short time in most cases, and being revived to tell the tale another day.

Catalyzed by such an event, the subject usually gets taken out of his/her body. The soul detaches and goes on a journey into another realm. Usually, *time* in the other place is described as *timeless.* Communication is instant and wordless. Love permeates everything. Light is omnipresent, not single sourced, not a solar occurrence.

Some time, usually minutes after the event on our timeline, a doctor or paramedic revives the patient, who lives to tell the tale another day.

There are hundreds and hundreds of such interviews on YouTube. It is highly recommended that you watch some. Listen to what these people are saying. Notice the patterns, how the subjects speak. **It’s not about believing them.** And of course, fraud has always been a concern in these areas of investigation.

But nothing is real and everything is out of phase.
