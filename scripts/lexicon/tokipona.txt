# words
## Particles and grammar words
a | ah, oh (emotion) | particle
anu | or (in questions) | particle
e | marks the direct object | particle
en | and (joins subjects) | particle
la | marks context: if, when | particle
li | separates subject from verb | not after mi or sina alone
o | hey!; marks a command or wish | particle
pi | of (regroups modifiers) | particle
taso | but; only
kin | also, indeed, too | ku suli
n | hmm (thinking) | ku suli

# phrases
## Greetings
toki! | hello!
toki pona! | hello! (literally good talk)
mi pona. | I'm well.
sina pona ala pona? | are you well? | X ala X makes a yes/no question
pona! | thanks! good!
pona tawa sina. | thanks to you; good to you
tawa pona! | goodbye (to someone leaving), have a good journey
awen pona! | goodbye (to someone staying)
lape pona! | sleep well!
moku pona! | enjoy your meal!
kama pona! | welcome!
mi pilin pona. | I feel good.
mi pilin ike. | I feel bad.
mi pakala. | I'm sorry; my mistake.
pakala! | damn! oops!
ike a! | oh no!
## Getting to know someone
nimi sina li seme? | what's your name?
nimi mi li Wiki. | my name is Wiki. | names are proper adjectives: jan Wiki
mi jan Wiki. | I'm Wiki.
sina tan ma seme? | where are you from?
mi tan ma ni. | I'm from this country.
sina lon ma seme? | where are you?
mi lon tomo. | I'm at home.
sina pali e seme? | what do you do?
sina sona ala sona toki pona? | do you know Toki Pona?
mi sona lili e toki pona. | I know a little Toki Pona.
mi kama sona e toki pona. | I'm learning Toki Pona. | kama sona = come to know
toki pona li pona tawa mi. | I like Toki Pona. | X li pona tawa mi = I like X
## Understanding
mi sona ala. | I don't know; I don't understand.
sina ken ala ken toki sin? | can you say it again?
ni li seme? | what is this?
nimi ni li seme? | what does this word mean?
mi sona. | I understand; I know.
lon. | yes; true.
ala. | no.
lon a! | that's right!
ken. | maybe.
ni li pona. | this is good.
ni li ike. | this is bad.
ni li nasa! | that's weird!

sina pona mute! | you're great!

# sentences
mi moku. | I eat; I'm eating. | no li after mi
sina pona. | You are good.
jan li moku. | The person eats. | li separates subject and predicate
ona li suli. | He/she is big; it is important.
mi moku e kili. | I eat fruit. | e marks the object
jan li lukin e waso. | The person sees the bird.
mi jan pona sina. | I am your friend. | jan pona = friend
tomo mi li lili. | My house is small. | modifiers follow the noun
mi tawa tomo. | I go home. | tawa as a verb, no preposition needed
mi tawa ma tomo. | I'm going to the city. | ma tomo = city
mi lon tomo. | I am at home. | lon = to be at
soweli li lape lon supa. | The animal sleeps on the bed.
mi pana e lipu tawa sina. | I give the book to you. | tawa = to (a recipient)
mi pali kepeken ilo. | I work with a tool. | kepeken = using
mi wile moku. | I want to eat. | wile before a verb
mi ken toki e toki pona. | I can speak Toki Pona. | ken before a verb
mi kama sona e toki pona. | I'm learning Toki Pona.
mi sona ala e nimi ni. | I don't know this word. | ala follows what it negates
sina moku ala moku? | Do you eat? / Are you eating? | verb ala verb = yes/no question
sina wile e seme? | What do you want? | seme replaces the unknown part
jan seme li kama? | Who is coming?
tenpo pini la mi lili. | In the past, I was small. | context + la
tenpo kama la mi tawa ma ante. | In the future I'll go to another country.
tenpo ni la mi pali. | Now I am working.
sina kama la mi pilin pona. | If you come, I'll be happy. | a clause before la gives the condition
telo li kama tan sewi. | Water comes from the sky; it's raining.
suno li seli. | The sun is hot.
tenpo pimeja li kama. | Night is coming.
mi en sina li jan pona. | You and I are friends. | en joins subjects
mi moku e kili e pan. | I eat fruit and bread. | repeat e for several objects
ona li moku li lape. | He eats and sleeps. | repeat li for several predicates
jan pi toki pona li mute. | Toki Pona speakers are many. | pi regroups: person of good-language
tomo pi jan pona mi li suli. | My friend's house is big.
lipu ni li pona tawa mi. | I like this book. | pona tawa mi = good to me
mi olin e mama mi. | I love my parent(s).
o moku! | Eat! | o + verb = command
jan Wiki o, kama! | Wiki, come here! | name + o = calling someone
mi wile e ni: sina pona. | I want this: that you are well. | ni: introduces a clause
mi sona e ni: ona li jan pona. | I know that he is a good person.
mi pilin e ni: ni li ike. | I feel that this is bad.
ilo sona li pona tawa pali. | Computers are good for work. | ilo sona = computer
mi kepeken ilo toki. | I'm using a phone. | ilo toki = phone
kalama musi li pona tawa mi. | I like music. | kalama musi = music
mi wile tawa tomo moku. | I want to go to a restaurant. | tomo moku = restaurant
jan lili li musi. | The child is playing. | jan lili = child
ona li pakala e ilo. | He broke the tool.
mi awen lon tomo. | I'm staying at home.
nasin ni li tawa ma tomo. | This road goes to the city.
jan ale li jan sama. | All people are siblings. | jan sama = sibling
toki pona li toki lili. | Toki Pona is a small language.
