# sentences
Yo soy estudiante. | I am a student. | ser for identity and profession
Ella está en casa. | She is at home. | estar for location
El café está caliente. | The coffee is hot. | estar for a temporary state
Tengo dos hermanos. | I have two brothers. | hermanos can mean brothers or siblings
Me gusta el chocolate. | I like chocolate. | gustar: the thing liked is the subject
Nos gustan los libros. | We like books. | plural subject, so gustan
¿Dónde vives? | Where do you live?
Vivimos cerca del mar. | We live near the sea. | de + el = del
Hoy hace calor. | It's hot today. | weather uses hacer
Mañana va a llover. | It's going to rain tomorrow. | ir a + infinitive for the near future
Voy a la playa los domingos. | I go to the beach on Sundays. | a + la; los domingos = on Sundays
El tren sale a las ocho. | The train leaves at eight. | a las + hour
¿Puedes ayudarme? | Can you help me? | the pronoun attaches to the infinitive
No puedo venir esta noche. | I can't come tonight.
Quiero aprender español. | I want to learn Spanish. | querer + infinitive
Tengo que trabajar mañana. | I have to work tomorrow. | tener que = to have to
Estoy leyendo un libro. | I'm reading a book. | estar + gerund for actions in progress
¿Qué estás haciendo? | What are you doing?
Mi hermana es más alta que yo. | My sister is taller than me. | más … que
Este es el mejor restaurante de la ciudad. | This is the best restaurant in the city. | superlative uses de
Hay mucha gente en la calle. | There are a lot of people in the street. | hay = there is / there are
No hay nada en la nevera. | There's nothing in the fridge. | double negative is normal
Nunca bebo café por la noche. | I never drink coffee at night.
Ayer comí paella. | Yesterday I ate paella. | preterite for a finished action
Fuimos al cine el sábado. | We went to the cinema on Saturday. | fuimos is the preterite of ir (and ser)
Cuando era niño, vivía en el campo. | When I was a child, I lived in the countryside. | imperfect for background and habits
He visitado México dos veces. | I have visited Mexico twice. | present perfect: haber + participle
¿Has comido? | Have you eaten?
Llevo tres años aquí. | I've been here for three years. | llevar + time
Hace dos años que estudio español. | I've been studying Spanish for two years. | hace + time + que + present
Se lo di a mi madre. | I gave it to my mother. | le becomes se before lo
Dímelo. | Tell me it. | pronouns attach to positive commands
No me lo digas. | Don't tell me (it). | pronouns go before negative commands
Espero que estés bien. | I hope you're well. | esperar que + subjunctive
Quiero que vengas. | I want you to come. | different subject, so subjunctive
Cuando llegues, llámame. | When you arrive, call me. | cuando + subjunctive for the future
Si tengo tiempo, iré. | If I have time, I'll go. | si + present, future in the result
Si tuviera dinero, viajaría más. | If I had money, I would travel more. | imperfect subjunctive + conditional
Me duele la cabeza. | My head hurts. | doler works like gustar
Se habla español aquí. | Spanish is spoken here. | impersonal se
Acabo de llegar. | I've just arrived. | acabar de + infinitive
Vuelvo a intentarlo. | I'll try again. | volver a + infinitive = do again
Lo importante es intentarlo. | The important thing is to try. | lo + adjective = the … thing
¿Te importa si abro la ventana? | Do you mind if I open the window?
Sería mejor esperar. | It would be better to wait. | conditional
Me lo pasé muy bien. | I had a great time. | pasarlo bien = to have a good time
Por fin terminé el trabajo. | I finally finished the work.
Estudio para aprender, no para aprobar. | I study to learn, not to pass. | para + infinitive = in order to
Gracias por tu ayuda. | Thanks for your help. | por for cause or exchange
Es posible que llegue tarde. | I might arrive late. | es posible que + subjunctive
No sabía que hablabas francés. | I didn't know you spoke French. | imperfect in reported speech
Ojalá pudiera ir contigo. | I wish I could go with you. | ojalá + imperfect subjunctive
Cuanto más practico, mejor hablo. | The more I practise, the better I speak. | cuanto más …, más/mejor …
